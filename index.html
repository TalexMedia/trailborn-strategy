How Trailborn Hotels & Resorts could use it

Trailborn could build a documentary series around people whose work is tied to its destinations. A film could follow a local resident's relationship with the coast around Mendocino or the outdoors around Williams, letting that person's experience shape the story. The same approach could continue in another Trailborn destination, with a different person and a different account of life there.

Why a documentary series fits Trailborn's destinations
Place is part of Trailborn's identity. The brand describes its hotels as telling the story of their location. A documentary could explore that connection through a person's experience.
The destinations offer different subjects. Mendocino's coast and Highlands' mountain setting could give successive films different surroundings while keeping the focus on a person's relationship with a place.
Trailborn connects a stay to things to do. Its restaurants and excursions could inform the choice of documentary subjects. The films could explore interests travelers could pursue during a visit.

How Trailborn Hotels & Resorts could use it

Trailborn could use a short scripted comedy to explore the different ways people want to spend an outdoor getaway. The humor could come from choosing between an active day and time to relax, with returning characters bringing different preferences to those decisions. Trailborn's combination of outdoor destinations and activities at the hotel could inform the situations across the series.

Why a travel comedy could fit Trailborn
The stay offers choices beyond the outdoor excursion. Jackson Hole lists pool days, duckpin bowling and live music. That variety could give a comedy specific choices for its characters to disagree about.
Relaxation is part of Trailborn's offer. Its About page describes time to unwind after an adventure. A comedy could find humor in different ideas about the right balance of activity and rest.
Trailborn already has social channels. Its official directory links to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, where short episodes could become a recurring part of the brand's video output.
03 Lead With Value

The Legendeering Framework

The methodology behind every Talex production, and behind the two concepts above. Five directives, one core principle.

Legendeering is Talex's proprietary strategy. The core principle is straightforward: lead with value, always. The brand earns trust by giving without expectation, and that trust grows over time because people keep choosing to watch.

Five directives drive it:

  • Give Without Expectation. Value delivered without strings. The kind of generosity that builds loyalty money cannot buy.
  • Be Relentlessly Authentic. Say true things. Show real work. Speak like a human being.
  • It's Not About You. It's About Them. The audience has to come first. It's all about their wants and needs and making something they will choose to watch.
  • Start With Story, Follow With Facts. Story gives information shape, context, and emotional weight. Facts alone rarely move people.
  • Consistency Is Key. Trust is built in moments, repeated over time.

Legendeering is not limited to the formats described above. Talex produces video for a variety of clients, across a range of industries in all available formats.
